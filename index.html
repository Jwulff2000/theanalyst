Work project · anonymised

Prior Authorisation Process Improvement

Problem
A fragmented prior-authorisation process at a regional health plan relied on phone, fax, email and spreadsheets. This caused delayed decisions, manual re-entry, limited status visibility and mismatched authorisation references in claims. The goal was to define requirements for a safer, traceable and more efficient end-to-end process.
My contribution
Contributed business analysis across the project: defined the problem statement, mapped the current and future process, identified stakeholders and gaps, analysed root causes, and documented business, functional, non-functional and transition requirements. Developed acceptance criteria, KPIs, risks, assumptions and a requirements traceability approach.
Approach
Stakeholder analysis, elicitation planning, AS-IS and TO-BE process mapping, gap analysis, Five Whys, fishbone analysis, MoSCoW prioritisation, requirements traceability, KPI definition and risk analysis. Health tech considerations: interoperability, privacy, role-based access, audit trails and clinical governance.
Outcome
A consolidated Business Analysis and Business Requirements Report recommending a shared case record, unique reference, structured intake, urgency-based routing, decision notifications, claims-reference validation and operational reporting.
Request the project report

Closed-Loop Medication and Pharmacy Optimisation

Problem
In a regional health network, disconnected prescribing, pharmacy, inventory, medication-administration and billing processes created potential patient-safety risks, stockouts, expiry-related waste, incomplete billing, manual workload and limited reporting. The goal was to define a phased, integrated closed-loop medication-management solution.
My contribution
Contributed business analysis: identified and analysed stakeholders, developed engagement and communication artefacts, documented current problems and root-cause themes, supported the business case, and defined requirements, risks, assumptions, dependencies, KPIs and phased implementation considerations.
Approach
Stakeholder mapping, Power-Interest analysis, RACI, stakeholder register, communication planning, current-state process and root-cause analysis, requirements elicitation, business-case analysis, KPI definition, RAID management and benefits tracking. Hybrid Agile with formal clinical-safety, privacy, security and go-live gates.
Outcome
A business case and stakeholder analysis recommending a phased platform that integrates electronic prescribing, pharmacy verification, inventory, eMAR, barcode medication administration, billing reconciliation and analytics. It strengthened my ability to apply business analysis to safety-critical healthcare transformation.
Request the project report
Health tech innovation / hackathon · anonymised

AI-Enabled Readmission Decision Support

Problem
After the merger of two hospital organisations, 30-day readmission risk was hard to manage: inconsistent discharge workflows, duplicate patient identities, variable data quality, unclear decision ownership and the clinical-safety risks of using AI without human oversight. The goal was a safe, governed workflow that uses AI to prioritise follow-up while keeping clinical decisions with authorised professionals.
My contribution
Led the team's business analysis delivery: stakeholder analysis, RACI, current- and future-state process mapping, requirements, AI decision-governance design, use cases, traceability and KPI design. Facilitated the translation of clinical, operational, data, privacy and technology needs into an end-to-end proposal, with mandatory human review, override and audit controls.
Approach
Stakeholder mapping, RACI, AS-IS and TO-BE mapping, business, functional, non-functional and transition requirements, use cases, acceptance criteria, traceability matrix, KPI definition, data-quality analysis, privacy-by-design, AI governance, human-in-the-loop design, risk analysis and pilot-transition planning.
Outcome
A solution pack for an AI-enabled readmission-risk and follow-up workflow: patient-identity reconciliation, data validation, explainable risk scoring, mandatory clinical review for high-risk or uncertain outputs, documented clinician overrides, and audit and model-governance monitoring.
Request the solution pack
